# Patrick Fanella / Patrick__eff

I'm Patrick Fanella, a senior full-stack and backend engineer in Chicago. I build web products and the systems behind them, with a focus on search, media, and tools for independent culture.

At DeliverZero, I built inventory, returns, and partner integrations across three markets, reducing manual operational work by approximately 25%. Through [SUBCULT](https://github.com/subculture-collective), I build products for finding clips, searching recording archives, making music visuals and running community projects. The source for all of them is public.

**Open to senior full-stack, backend, and product engineering roles.**

[Portfolio](https://patrickfanella.co) · [Resume](https://patrickfanella.co/resume) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/patrick-fanella/) · [Email](mailto:patrick@subcult.tv)

**Core stack:** Go · Python · TypeScript · React · PostgreSQL · PostGIS · OpenSearch · Docker · Kubernetes

### Featured work

- **[clpr](https://github.com/subculture-collective/clpr)**: find Twitch clips by creator, topic and tag, and keep playlists to share. Go, React, PostgreSQL and hybrid search. [clpr.tv](https://clpr.tv)
- **[HasanAra](https://github.com/subculture-collective/hasanara)** on **[Rekolekt](https://github.com/subculture-collective/rekolekt)**: search years of broadcasts by phrase and open each result at its timestamp. FastAPI, React, GPU transcription, PostgreSQL and OpenSearch. Transcripts are automatic, so check the recording before quoting. [hasanara.tv](https://hasanara.tv)
- **[Headcount](https://github.com/subculture-collective/headcount)**: scores the Twitch top 200 for bot-like viewer and chat patterns. Go, TimescaleDB and statistical scoring. A signal, not proof. [headcount.subcult.tv](https://headcount.subcult.tv/)
- **[Undertow](https://github.com/subculture-collective/undertow)**: a layered music visualizer with timed lyrics that exports MP4 in the browser. [undertow.subcult.tv](https://undertow.subcult.tv)

### Products and apps

- **[Kork](https://github.com/subculture-collective/kork)**: free link pages; payment buttons go to your own accounts. [kork.subcult.tv](https://kork.subcult.tv/)
- **[Patchwork](https://github.com/subculture-collective/patchwork)**: a pre-alpha AT Protocol demo where neighbors ask for and offer local help. Demo only. [patchwork.subcult.tv](https://patchwork.subcult.tv)
- **[Subcult OS](https://github.com/subculture-collective/subcult-os)**: event planning, staffing, free reservations and door check-in for small shows. In development. [os.subcult.tv](https://os.subcult.tv/)
- **[Prichal](https://github.com/subculture-collective/prichal)**: managed hosting for your own Bluesky account server. Private preview. [prichal.subcult.tv](https://prichal.subcult.tv)
- **[Left Field](https://github.com/subculture-collective/left-field)**: seat-level election research with sourced records and provisional, versioned scores. [left-field.subcult.tv](https://left-field.subcult.tv/)
- **[Clustr](https://github.com/subculture-collective/clustr)**: maps Reddit communities by shared users as a 3D network, with a [Unity client](https://github.com/subculture-collective/clustr-galaxy). [clustr.subcult.tv](https://clustr.subcult.tv)
- **[Chatty Cat](https://github.com/subculture-collective/chatty-cat)**: a self-hosted Twitch chat client with merged and split channel panes. [chat.subcult.tv](https://chat.subcult.tv)
- **[Backline](https://github.com/subculture-collective/backline)**: a Discord bot and dashboard for onboarding, moderation, tickets and feeds.
- **[Tadooer](https://github.com/subculture-collective/tadooer)**: a self-hosted task and calendar planner for one person.
- **[Edda](https://github.com/subculture-collective/edda)**: a tabletop roleplaying game with a language-model game master. [edda.subcult.tv](https://edda.subcult.tv/)
- **[JuryRigged](https://github.com/subculture-collective/jury-rigged)**: a courtroom drama played by language models, with audience votes.
- **[Artemis Mission Hub](https://github.com/subculture-collective/artemis)**: NASA Artemis schedules, updates, media and live trajectory. [artemis.subcult.tv](https://artemis.subcult.tv/)
- **[Commons](https://github.com/subculture-collective/commons)**: a hub for finding and building open alternatives to familiar software. [commons.subcult.tv](https://commons.subcult.tv/)
- **[Roberts Rules](https://github.com/subculture-collective/roberts-rules)**: a simulator and live floor for chair-led parliamentary meetings. Early stage.
- **[dsa-signal](https://github.com/subculture-collective/dsa-signal)**: members-only encrypted chat on a private Matrix server. In development.
- **[The Common House](https://github.com/subculture-collective/the-common-house)**: a narrative investigation game set at a radio outpost. Early prototype.
- **[Mantik](https://github.com/subculture-collective/mantik)**: a terminal-first paper-trading research system with synthetic demo data.
- **[Deadweb Relay](https://github.com/subculture-collective/deadweb-relay)**: an old-web homepage with writable notes and feeds, plus a [log observatory](https://github.com/subculture-collective/www2-observatory). [www2.onnwee.me](https://www2.onnwee.me/)
